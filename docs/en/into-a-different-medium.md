### / Philosophy

It seems to take less and less to decide that we know someone.

A résumé, a few labels, a short introduction.

We ask what they’re good at, what they’ve done, and what they can bring. We rarely get to sit down and hear **why they wanted to make the thing in the first place.**

As technology keeps moving, we want to pay attention to the people behind it again.

The person who stayed up all night because of a strange idea. The person who took apart what they’d made and started over. The one with a notebook full of ideas who hasn’t found anyone to make them with yet.

Nobody has to fit our idea of an exceptional person. Nobody has to prove they belong by showing us something spectacular.

**Before we are makers, we are people.**

---

### / Narrative　Into a Different Medium

Light can change direction when it passes from one medium into another.

Maybe people can, too.

Most of the time, we follow our own paths. We bring different experiences, habits and ways of looking at the world.

**We might never have known each other.**

Then one day, someone who’s never touched hardware sits down next to a mechanical engineer. A question from a designer makes a programmer look at their code again. An idea nobody paid much attention to finds someone at another table who wants to see it work.

Nobody knows where those encounters will lead. That’s what we’re here to find out.

We want to create **a different medium**: a place where paths that wouldn’t normally cross can meet, and where an idea that lived in one person’s head can become something two people make together.

We’re not asking everyone to leave in the same direction. We hope you’ll leave with something in your world that wouldn’t have been there otherwise.

**Some things would never happen if our worlds never met.**

### / Highlights

#### 01 · OPEN EXPO

**Every team deserves a place to show its work.**

On the morning of Day 5, every team that submits a project will have its own booth. Visitors can try the projects for themselves and talk with the people who made them.

The expo isn’t just for the winners. There’s room for the experiments and the prototypes that aren’t quite finished, too.

#### 02 · LIGHTNING INTERVIEW

**Ten minutes, starting with something you made.**

Creators can choose to book a short interview with an industry partner, bring a project and talk about the part they worked on, what went wrong, and how they figured out what to do next.

A conversation about what you might do next doesn’t have to start with a résumé.

#### 03 · CROSS-TEAM EXCHANGE

**Leave time for the next table.**

See what another team is trying to solve and ask why they approached it that way. Show them the part of your own project you haven’t figured out.

What changes your project might not be another line of code. It might be one question from someone else.

#### 04 · BEYOND REFR/ACT

**The room doesn’t have to be where it ends.**

We’ll share projects, conversations and creators’ stories through the event website and creator communities in the U.S., China and elsewhere. People who couldn’t come in person can still get to know the people making things here.
