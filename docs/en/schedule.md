# REFR/ACT event schedule

[Back to the playbook](../../README.md) / [中文](../zh/schedule.md)

**Day 1 is setup. Days 2–5 are in person.** This edition is open to creators regardless of school affiliation, student status, or discipline. We’re planning for 300–500 people near Princeton, New Jersey, tentatively in late March 2027. We’ll post the exact dates, venue, participant registration and detailed times here once confirmed. [Organizing team recruitment](../../README.md#join-the-2027-organizing-team) is separate from participant registration.

Looking for the [PRISMS House Event](prisms-house-event.md)? It is a separate school game competition with its own schedule and submission rules.

| Day | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 · Setup | Organizers and partners set up, test equipment, the network and the submission flow, and prepare technical desks and demo tables. |
| Day 2 · Opening | Check in, go over the submission and judging rules, form teams and start a prototype. |
| Day 3 · Build | Keep a substantial block for building, with mentor support, optional workshops and voluntary product trials. We’re also planning time for teams to try each other’s unfinished prototypes before submission. |
| Day 4 · Expo and first round | Submit before the cutoff, then demo at your table. Judges run the first round under the published rules. Optional lightning interviews take place in a separate area. |
| Day 5 · Finals | Finalists demo and take questions, followed by awards and closing. |

Bring your code or hardware to the technical desk on Day 3. During the expo on Day 4, show people what they can try and explain what still does not work. On Day 5, finalists demo the core flow and answer judges' questions. Meeting a partner or taking an interview does not count toward a score.

Teams may choose their own problems. Partner challenges and interviews are optional. See the [partnership guide](partnerships.md) and [operations playbook](operations.md).
