# REFR/ACT event schedule

[Back to the playbook](../../README.md) / [中文](../zh/schedule.md)

**Day 1 is setup. Days 2–5 are in person.** This edition is for campus communities and is not limited to one school. Full eligibility and supervision requirements will be published with registration. We’re planning for **about 500 creators** in the Princeton area, New Jersey, in **spring 2027**. Participant registration is not open yet. We’ll post the exact dates, venue, participant signup and detailed times here once confirmed. [Organizing team recruitment](../../README.md#join-the-2027-organizing-team) is separate from participant registration.

Looking for the [PRISMS House Event](prisms-house-event.md)? It is a separate school game competition with its own schedule and submission rules.

| Day | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 · Setup | Organizers and partners set up, test equipment, the network and the submission flow, and prepare technical desks and demo tables. |
| Day 2 · Opening | Check in, go over the submission and judging rules, form teams and start a prototype. |
| Day 3 · Build | Keep a substantial block for building, with mentor support, optional workshops and voluntary product trials. We’re also planning time for teams to try each other’s unfinished prototypes before submission. |
| Day 4 · Submissions and first-round judging | Submit before the cutoff. Judges run the first round under the published rules. |
| Day 5 · Open Expo and finals | In the morning, every team that submits a project has its own booth at the Open Expo. Finals, Q&A, awards and closing follow. |

Bring your code or hardware to the technical desk on Day 3. At the Open Expo on the morning of Day 5, show people what they can try and explain what still does not work. Finalists demo the core flow and answer judges’ questions later that day. Meeting a partner or taking an interview does not count toward a score.

Lightning interviews are opt-in and run in a separate area. Interview times will be announced. Teams may choose their own problems; partner challenges are optional. See the [partnership guide](partnerships.md) and [operations playbook](operations.md).
