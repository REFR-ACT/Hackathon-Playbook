# Open tracks: partners set the direction, teams choose what to build

[Playbook home](../../README.md) / [中文](../zh/tracks.md)

A company track offers a direction to explore. For example, **“Everyday devices that understand context”** leaves teams to decide what a device should notice, whom it should help, and what to build. There is no fixed assignment or required sponsor product. Teams may choose whether to use any equipment, tools, or technical support offered separately.

## How many tracks can a project enter?

**A single project can enter multiple tracks.** Read the rules published before the event and choose the tracks relevant to your work. Follow each track’s submission requirements and be evaluated separately in each. You may also enter the main competition without entering a company track. Track entry and use of partner tools do not affect main competition scores.

## Who sets the rules and awards?

| Who | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Company partner | Propose one open direction and set the submission requirements, judging rules, and track award. The company funds its award separately with cash or equivalent products and appoints judges to apply the published rules. |
| Organizers | Publish the company-credited track description and rules in advance, handle entry and submission, and organize demos. Main competition judging uses its own common rubric. |
| Participant teams | Choose the problem, approach, and form of the work. When entering more than one track, follow each track’s requirements and take part in its separate review. |

Entering a track does not transfer ownership of your work. The organizers share project materials or personal information only with permission. See [partnerships](partnerships.md) for how companies participate and what they receive afterward.
